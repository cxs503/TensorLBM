## 5. CAVEATS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### G3: Per-bin Closure
  1. The warmup window (6000 steps) is used to develop the droplet
     slip field, but the mass balance during this transient period
     shows non-zero closure error.
  2. This is a known limitation documented in the library:
     - warmup window closure error < 5e-2 (5%) is expected
     - actual measured max: 3.03% (within documented bound)
  3. The production window closure is extremely small (2.70e-17),
     indicating excellent mass conservation in steady state.

### G11a: Solver Consistency
  1. The n_f discrepancy is purely from input reconstruction,
     not solver logic (bitwise-identical with same inputs).
  2. The panel export does not close the panel n_f consistently.
  3. Area reconstruction is the source of the gap.
  4. This requires recalibration by the owner.
