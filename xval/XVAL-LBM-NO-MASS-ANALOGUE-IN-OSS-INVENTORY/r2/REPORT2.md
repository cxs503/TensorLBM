# XVAL-LBM-NO-MASS-ANALOGUE-IN-OSS-INVENTORY — round 2: the missing reference, and a real solver-adjacent defect

*Repo: TensorLBM · anchor: `XVAL-LBM-NO-MASS-ANALOGUE-IN-OSS-INVENTORY` · round 1: mission m21062 (#2429) + re-run (#2556) · this round: mission m22617*

## 0. What round 1 left open, and what round 2 answers

Round 1 re-executed m21062, showed its `C_I = 2.16/2.10/1.99` was not reproducible, and
recorded **WITHHELD** with four remediation items (spin-up gate, acoustic detuning,
same-mask/refined reference, Guo half-force). It also stated the one thing it could not do:

> "未做/无法判定：粘性（Bessel / 振荡 Stokes）精确解未实现" — a companion round
> (`XVAL-LBM-ADDEDMASS-KC-RESPONSE-ENVELOPE`) lists the same hole and records
> honest-FAIL against the **inviscid** potential anchors (+55 … +131 %).

Round 2 closes exactly that hole. Two findings, one defect:

* **D1 (defect, fixed).** `oscillatory_cylinder_inertia._interior_nodes` used a
  *4-neighbour* stencil to pick the solid core whose populations are zeroed. Streaming
  reaches first neighbours only, so a solid cell that is merely *diagonally* adjacent to
  fluid still injects its zeroed population into the fluid every step. In the
  `base`-family arms this produced `max|rho-1| = 0.30` and a fluid-mass drift of
  `-9 %` per window — the "near-wall compressibility / mass-budget artefact" that round 1
  attributed to the *case parameters*. With the 8-neighbour stencil: `max|rho-1| = 0.011`,
  `|dM|/M = 3e-7`, and `C_I` moves by 4 %. Fixed in `src/`, with two regression tests.
* **D2 (missing reference, implemented).** The right reference for a *linear-response*
  force measurement at finite `beta` is **not** the inviscid potential value. The exact
  unsteady-Stokes (1851) hydrodynamic function
  `Gamma = 1 + 4 K_1(z)/(z K_0(z))`, `z = (1+i) R sqrt(omega/nu)`, gives
  `C_I = Re Gamma`, `C_Q = -Im Gamma`, and `Re Gamma -> 1` only as `beta -> inf`.
  With it, the "+55 … +131 %" excess of round 1 is accounted for as the viscous part of
  the added mass, and the same measurements land at
  **+10.7 %** (E1, primary arm) instead of
  +65.4 %.

## ① Case

Fixed circular cylinder of reference diameter `D` in a doubly periodic D2Q9 box of side
`L = box*D`, driven by a uniform harmonic body force `a(t) = a0 cos(omega t)` on fluid
nodes (second-order Guo source); no moving boundary, no pressure gradient. Similarity
numbers: `KC = 2 pi u0/(omega D)`, `beta = D^2/(nu T)`, `Ma = u0 sqrt(3)`; Stokes-layer
thickness `delta = sqrt(2 nu/omega)` is quoted **in lattice cells** because it is the
resolution parameter that controls the error (fig. 2). Two independent force estimators
(E1 Ladd momentum exchange, E2 pressure+viscous traction) plus an exact third one
(E3 global momentum balance with the Guo half-force correction) are decomposed into the
first harmonic; `C_I` is the in-phase (added-mass) and `C_Q` the quadrature (viscous)
coefficient.

Primary arm: `D = 16`, `box = 4D` (`nx = 64`), `beta = 50`, `tau = 0.577`, `omega = 0.0314`
(`omega D = 0.50`, 102 acoustic linewidths from the box eigenfrequency), `KC = 0.11`,
`Ma = 0.017`.

## ② How

1. **Protocol gate (not a fixed transient).** Cosine ramp-on of the drive over 3 periods
   (a step in the body force rings the box acoustically for ~1/(nu k^2) steps), then the
   measurement window opens only when the trailing 3-period first-harmonic amplitude of
   the in-phase force stops moving to 0.5 % per period. A fixed 40-period spin-up arm is
   run as a control.
2. **Reference = potential (same mask, same domain, 2x refined grid, Richardson) x
   `Gamma_re(beta)`.** The factorisation is legitimate here because
   `delta = 1.8` cells against a wall gap of 24 cells, i.e. the Stokes layer never sees
   the periodic boundary; the confinement is carried entirely by the potential factor,
   and that factor is measured, not assumed (`C_a = 0.968` at this mask, `0.941` at 2x
   refinement, i.e. the reference itself carries 2.9 % grid error).
3. **Three estimators must agree**, `|E1-E3| <= 0.001` in the in-phase channel on every
   healthy arm (E3 is an exact momentum statement, not a discretisation of the wall).
4. **Invariants as gates**, not as prose: fluid mass budget `|dM|/M <= 1e-6` per window,
   `max|rho-1| = O(Ma^2)`, harmonic fit residual, `U/u0` (a resonant acoustic response
   would show up as `U/u0 >> 1`; the healthy arms sit at 0.91-0.92).

## ⑤ Caveats

* **The 4 % A/B on `C_I`** (defect arm 1.626 vs
  1.557) is smaller than the density artefact suggests: the zero
  injection mainly costs mass conservation and the density field, and only ~5 % of the
  force. The defect is still material — `max|rho-1| = 0.30` and
  `dM/M = -9.0e-02` are not a defensible basis for any
  compressibility-sensitive claim.
* **The quadrature channel is not validated**: `C_Q` sits 29-38 % *below* `-Im Gamma`
  at every `beta`, monotonically worse as the layer thins; E3's quadrature is useless
  here (`C_Q = 0.93` from a `0.02 %`-level cancellation of `a0 N_f`). Recorded as
  WITHHELD, hypothesis = staircase surface area for the tangential traction plus a
  finite-domain effect on the history term. Do not quote `C_Q` from this module.
* **`beta >= 200` is not reachable with BGK**: `nu = D^2/(beta T)` gives `tau = 0.538` at
  `beta = 100` and `tau = 0.519` at `beta = 400`, i.e. the potential limit cannot be
  approached by this route at `D <= 48` (MFA/TRT collision needed). The viscous correction
  is therefore an irreducible feature of what this module measures, not a bug.
* **Resolution, not the estimator, sets the error**: `+6.0 / +10.7 / +14.6 %` at
  `delta = 2.55 / 1.81 / 1.28` cells. Extrapolating on that trend (`delta >= 3.5` cells)
  is what a 3 %-caliber claim would need; that is the next ledger item.
* The `nx = 128` ladder (D=32, beta 25/50/100, 40-period control, box=3, KC-halved,
  int4 A/B) was submitted as cluster job **cj4267**; at report time it was still queued
  (queue depth > 40). This report's numbers are the local `nx = 64` ladder, which is
  inside the local short-test envelope; the generator for the `nx = 128` ladder is
  committed and runnable unchanged (`--arms ...`).
