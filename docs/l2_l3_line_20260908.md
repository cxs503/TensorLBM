## 1. The line in one paragraph

The owner-locked direction (2026-08-24) is a **real-time drag surrogate for
ship design, underwater vehicles first**. L1 — accuracy inside a fixed
parameter family — was already achieved by the v3 physics-geometry
conditioner (joint random-split MAPE cut from 3.61 % to **0.66 %**;
[drag_surrogate_fno_g2_20260824.md](drag_surrogate_fno_g2_20260824.md)).
The week of 2026-09-04..09-08 delivered the next two rungs: **L2**, where
geometry stops being a parameter row and arrives as an SDF (or an STL from
an external CAD), and **L3**, where a designer-closed loop drives the
surrogate over the design box. L2 ended the week working at noise level for
regenerable designs and with honest distance/sigma provenance for foreign
shapes. L3 ended the week with its first cut done — and, because the last
day purchased fresh LBM truth, with both of its improvement claims
**refuted as phantoms**. The headline of the arc is that the platform
refused to certify those phantoms *before* the truth existed: the
uncertainty machinery flagged exactly the region the truth later refuted.
Closed-loop optimization needs calibrated distrust, and the stack has it.

## 2. L2 — geometry leaves the parameter world

### 2.1 Reference-field sensitivity: is retrieval even sound? (2026-09-04)

The two-stage surrogate consumes a reference **field** plus an **SDF**. A
new geometry has no cached field, so L2 serving must borrow one from the
corpus. Before building that, the 2026-09-04 study asked the prior question:
*does it matter which in-corpus field we borrow?* Answer — no, within the
corpus manifold:

- Swapping the borrowed field between corpus donors (median rel input
  change 0.05–0.10) moves the ensemble mean by a **median 1.5e-6 to 1.3e-5
  relative** (max ~1.3e-3) — below the 8.3e-5 / 1.0e-4 (ts2/ts4)
  batch-composition fp noise floor of the same session.
- Swapping the **SDF** instead, holding the field donor fixed, costs
  **12.56 %** held-25 MAPE (ts2; 12.42 % ts4) = **x57.9** the baseline
  error; the sensitivity audit reads it as a 0.018-relative SDF input
  change producing a 0.12-relative output change.
- Sanity probe: an all-zero field (far outside the manifold — not a corpus
  swap) degrades to 1.66 % / 3.75 % (x7.7 / x17.9). The invariance is a
  property of the served function on this corpus, not a wiring artifact.

Conclusion: the frozen encoder’s geometry signal lives in the SDF; the
retrieved-field provider only has to be *in-manifold*, not *right*. That
is the license for everything below.

### 2.2 FieldProvider v1 + field-borrow serving wiring (#275, #278)

PR [#275](https://github.com/cxs503/TensorLBM/pull/275) added
`ai.field_provider` — `FieldProvider.from_corpus(view_dir)` builds the
406-row retrieval pool and answers `sdf_near` queries with strategy, donor
row and distance in the response. PR
[#278](https://github.com/cxs503/TensorLBM/pull/278) wired it into
`DragSurrogateService.predict(..., field_policy="field_borrow")`. The knob
is **opt-in**: the default `field_policy="cache"` path is byte-identical
to the pre-flag service, and every borrowed response carries the
`info["field_borrow"]` provenance block (strategy, donor, distance, guard
rel-L2 and threshold). Borrowed fields are never silent.

### 2.3 LODO end-to-end validation (2026-09-04): serving a never-simulated design

Five designs spanning the family spread — corpus-center mother (101),
blunt l/d=0.75 (100), slender l/d=1.30 (106, carries all 25 held rows),
appendage-scale LHS corner full/sail=3/fin=3 (94), and the vintage-offset
full/1/1 design (10) — each served with **every row of the design removed
from the retrieval pool** (pool = 406 rows minus the design; frozen
ensembles NOT retrained). Four arms per fold; the two that matter:

- **A `regen_borrowed`** — the full new-geometry path: SDF regenerated from
  CAD parameters, field borrowed `sdf_near` from the reduced pool.
- **B `oracle_own_stored`** — the same design’s own stored SDF and stored
  field (upper bound; optimistic where the target rows were in the
  training fit split).

Macro over the four regenerable designs (ts2): **A 0.2149 % vs B 0.2174 %
MAPE — a ratio of 0.989x** (ts4: 0.2029 % vs 0.2031 %, 0.999x). Per-design
ts2 ratios: 0.971 (94), 0.977 (106), 0.988 (101), 1.054 (100). The fully
held design 106 serves at **0.1535 % MAPE** through the complete path —
oracle-level, on a design whose truth was never in any pool the system
could see.

The SDF regeneration itself is **bit-exact for 121 of 122 corpus designs**
(worst rel-L2 = 0.0 for all of those). The single exception is design 10,
a vintage-provenance case (its stored mask disagrees with the current CAD
on 64/82 rows; regen rel-L2 0.0181 ≤ 1.8e-2). Its fold decomposes the
failure honestly: full path 12.09x oracle, but **own-field + regenerated
SDF is 12.05x while stored-SDF + borrowed field is 1.008x** — the damage
tracks the vintage SDF mismatch, not field borrowing.

**Conclusion**: for designs the CAD parameterization can regenerate,
serving a never-simulated design works at noise level, end to end.

### 2.4 STL ingest (#280): external CAD geometry enters the stack

PR [#280](https://github.com/cxs503/TensorLBM/pull/280) added the outward
(`write_mask_stl`, watertight boxel STL) and inward (`stl_to_sdf`,
re-voxelize + the corpus SDF chain: exact EDT, clip, pool) halves of the
STL interface. The 2026-09-05 campaign ran 8 designs (0, 1, 10, 94, 95,
100, 101, 106 — first design of every hull type plus global min/max
l/d) through the round trip:

- STL → mask **bit-equal 8/8** (`mask_xor_cells = 0`),
- mask → SDF **rel-L2 0.0, 8/8** (bit-equal vs the stored corpus SDFs),
- serving offset **0.0** — identical donors, identical fields, ensemble
  mean and every member shift exactly 0.0 through the full service.

An STL-sourced hull meets the surrogate under the identical input contract
as a corpus design.

### 2.5 Serving walkthrough (#283): the real service API, honestly

The capstone demo ([l2_serving_walkthrough_20260906.md]
(l2_serving_walkthrough_20260906.md), PR
[#283](https://github.com/cxs503/TensorLBM/pull/283)) pushed the held
design 106 through the **public** `predict` with an STL in hand:

- **MAPE 0.1535 %** (max APE 0.4017 %) over the 5-point Re sweep 66–591,
  2-sigma coverage 5/5; provenance: donor row 378 (design 105, the
  l/d=1.2 neighbour), retrieval distance 2.981, field guard pass at
  rel-L2 0.0876 vs the 0.15 threshold; cross-check vs the e2e A arm: same
  donor, per-row means agree to max rel 0.0.
- **Out-of-family probe** (volume-matched sphere, no truth): retrieval
  distance **x10.9** (32.58 vs 2.98), ensemble std inflated
  **x10.2–26.7** vs the in-family case, member band widening from
  ~0.55–1.25 % to ~24–34 % of C_D. **Both guards pass** — and that is
  not a bug: the field guard measures the *borrowed field* (a corpus row,
  in-manifold by construction) and the cond guard sees only the CAD
  descriptor triple. The out-of-family signal lives in **distance +
  sigma**, which is exactly what fired. The doc says this in so many
  words: the flags and the sigma ARE the deliverable of that leg.

### 2.6 Borrow cache + distance advisory (#285): making it fast, making it legible

PR [#285](https://github.com/cxs503/TensorLBM/pull/285) closed the two
followups the walkthrough left:

- **Per-geometry borrow-result cache**: median query latency
  **120.4 → 25.6 ms (x4.70)**, against a caller-fields baseline of
  **26.3 ms** measured in the same session (the retrieval it removes cost
  a median 63.9 ms alone; a cache hit answers in 0.34 ms). Accuracy is
  **bit-identical** (MAPE 0.153521… % unchanged, max rel deviation vs the
  walkthrough composition 0.0).
- **Out-of-family distance advisory**: calibrated on design-level
  leave-one-out nearest-neighbour SDF-L2 over the corpus (122 designs;
  median 1.124, p90 2.366). Threshold = the **corpus LOO max 8.875** —
  the corpus is a frozen finite population, so its design-LOO max IS the
  exact in-family envelope and the rule has **zero corpus false
  positives by construction** (a p90 multiple would have mislabelled the
  legitimate slender tail at 2.94–8.88). Design 106 sits at 2.981 =
  0.336x threshold (`in_family`); the walkthrough sphere at 32.576 =
  3.670x (`suspect`). The advisory changes **no served number** — it is
  labelling, not gating.

## 3. L3 — the loop closes (first cut, #286)

PR [#286](https://github.com/cxs503/TensorLBM/pull/286) ran the first
surrogate-driven design search: minimise C_D at Re = 200, hull `full`,
design variables (l_over_d_mult, sail_scale, fin_scale) in the corpus LHS
box l/d ∈ [0.75, 1.3], sail/fin ∈ [0.4, 3.0]. Budget: 80 coarse LHS +
6 refine rounds x 8 per arm + 14 validation = **190 surrogate queries in
73.4 s wall** (mean 350 ms/query). Two acquisition arms shared the coarse
stage: greedy (min predicted mean) and LCB (k = 1.0).

Both arms converged into the **corpus-best basin**: for each pick the
nearest truth-bearing corpus design at Re = 200 is the same design — row
233 (`full`, sail 0.4, fin 3.0, l/d 1.0; truth C_D 4.3817) — at box
distances 0.1775 (greedy) and 0.1934 (LCB).

| arm | l/d | sail | fin | pred C_D | ens std | LCB | guard | borrow dist |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| greedy | 0.919 | 0.646 | 2.916 | 4.2934 | 0.0811 | 4.2123 | reject | 3.027 |
| LCB k=1 | 0.902 | 0.600 | 3.000 | 4.2995 | 0.0927 | 4.2068 | reject | 3.606 |

Both arms claimed an improvement over the true best (greedy -2.01 %, LCB
-1.88 %) and both claims were **labeled uncertifiable in the PR itself**:
`mission_hull_truth_coverage` reports corpus truth for the mission hull at
l/d levels **[1.0] only** — any pick off l/d = 1.0 is out-of-family
extrapolation. The validation that WAS possible: the evaluator reproduces
the 15 corpus anchor rows at Re = 200 (14 designs) at **MAPE 0.443 %**
(max APE 1.632 %) — a reproduction check (anchors are in-pool and mostly
in the frozen members’ fit split), not generalization.

Ranking the two picks against each other was also honestly closed off:
the predicted gap (0.0061) is below the anchor reproduction error
(0.0262 mean abs) — *undecidable from existing truth*.

## 4. The adjudication (2026-09-08, fresh LBM) — the climax

The only way to certify an off-anchor optimum is fresh CFD. The
2026-09-08 campaign ran a 6-point, 4000-step scan on `cuda:4`
(**~145 s wall**, 6/6 completed, ~96–97 MLUPs/point): the control, both
picks, and three l/d bridge points. Labels under the **`cd_proj`**
convention (per-point projected area; `aproj = 165` at every point) —
the convention the corpus row itself uses.

### 4.1 The control gate: the harness reproduces the corpus bit-identically

The control point IS row 233’s design (sail 0.4, fin 3.0, l/d 1.0). Its
fresh scan returns **cd_proj = 4.381678732563599 — rel_diff = 0.0,
bitwise equal** to the corpus label (mask bit-equal to the stored corpus
mask into the bargain). Everything below is therefore measured against a
truth chain demonstrably identical to the one the surrogate was trained
on. (The alternative `cd_ref` convention differs by 1.39 % — adjudication
is `cd_proj`, per the gate.)

### 4.2 Both improvement claims are phantom

| arm | pick (l/d, sail, fin) | claimed | realized | truth C_D | claim error | surrogate APE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| greedy | 0.919, 0.646, 2.916 | -2.0149 % | **+0.9592 %** | 4.423708032873091 | -2.97 pp | 2.95 % |
| LCB k=1 | 0.902, 0.600, 3.000 | -1.8755 % | **+1.9260 %** | 4.466071227283187 | -3.80 pp | 3.73 % |

Both picks are **worse than doing nothing** (row 233). The surrogate’s APE
at the picks (2.95 % / 3.73 %) is ~7–8x its 0.443 % anchor-reproduction
MAPE — the error is not uniform noise, it concentrates exactly where the
loop was asked to extrapolate.

### 4.3 The truth l/d curve: the reward pointed the wrong way

At the greedy corner (sail 0.646, fin 2.916 held), the truth along l/d is
monotone: **4.4377 at 0.902, 4.4237 at 0.919, 4.3997 at 0.960, 4.3740 at
1.000** — OLS slope **dC_D/d(l/d) = -0.638**. Shortening the hull
**raises** C_D at Re = 200; the surrogate reward gradient pointed
opposite. (The sail/fin corner direction at l/d = 1.0 is mildly real:
point g10005 — the greedy corner’s sail/fin at l/d = 1.0 — truth
4.3739966982693375, -0.18 % vs row 233.)

### 4.4 The honesty contract worked

This is the section that matters for the product line. Every off-manifold
signal the platform carries fired **on the refuted picks, before the truth
existed**:

- ensemble std at the picks = **2.63x / 3.01x** the std at the true-best
  anchor design;
- borrow distance **3.03 / 3.61** vs **0.0** at the anchors (the anchors
  donate to themselves);
- the cond-space guard flagged **reject** on both picks;
- and the structural flag — `mission_hull_truth_coverage` said corpus
  truth covers l/d **[1.0] only**, so both sub-1.0 l/d claims were
  uncertifiable — and the uncertifiable region is **exactly** the refuted
  region.

The loop was prevented from certifying a phantom win by machinery that was
already in the response payload. That is the L3 lesson, bought for 145 s of
GPU time: **closed-loop optimization needs calibrated distrust, and the
stack has it.**

### 4.5 Byproduct: an anchor-ready k=3 set for l/d ~ 0.9

The bridge points make the refuted region cheaper to defend next time:
**brb0004 @ l/d 0.902, bra0003 @ 0.960, g10005 @ 1.0** (min / geo-mid /
max of the scanned range; the greedy pick itself is an interior 4th
redundancy anchor). Caveat carried in the json: the l/d span is **0.0448
decades**, far below the 0.4-decade spread magnitude of the #265/#266
k=3 recipe (which was formulated for log10-Re) — use the set with that
span caveat.

(Honest footnote from the gate: per-point tail drifts are 0.995e-4 to
1.20e-4 — five of six marginally above the 1e-4 mission expectation,
common-mode stationarity of the 4000-step budget at Re = 200. The control
still reproduces the corpus truth bit-identically, i.e. the corpus label
itself was produced with the same tail character, and all six drifts sit
within 2e-5 of each other, so the ratio comparisons are common-mode.)

## 5. Where the line stands — and what is next

**Standing, honestly:**

- **L2 works for regenerable designs at noise level**: the LODO full path
  is 0.989x oracle (macro, four regenerable designs), SDF regeneration is
  bit-exact 121/122, and the held design 106 serves at 0.1535 % MAPE
  through the public API.
- **L2 works for STL-sourced shapes with honest provenance**: bit-equal
  ingest (8/8), zero serving offset, and an out-of-family story told by
  distance + sigma (x10.9 / x10.2–26.7 on the sphere probe) rather than by
  guards that cannot see shape. Latency is caller-fields-equivalent
  (25.6 vs 26.3 ms median) with the cache.
- **L3 demonstrated basin-finding and honest labeling, nothing more**: the
  search reaches the corpus-best basin in 190 queries / 73.4 s, labels its
  own claims uncertifiable where truth is absent — and the adjudication
  confirmed both claims were indeed phantom. No certified design
  improvement has been produced by the loop yet.

**Open items, in order:**

1. **Truth coverage outside corpus manifolds.** The l/d < 1.0 lesson: add
   anchors BEFORE optimizing across unsupported axes. The k=3 recipe
   exists (the #265/#266 spread rule) and the anchor-ready set for
   l/d ~ 0.9 is already scanned (§4.5).
2. **Multi-Re objectives** — the first cut was single-Re,
   single-objective; the mission profile needs the sweep.
3. **Greedy-vs-LCB ranking needs truth-dense regions** — with the picks’
   predicted gap below the reproduction error, only fresh LBM or a denser
   anchor grid can rank acquisition arms.

The closing wave is in `main`: PRs
[#284](https://github.com/cxs503/TensorLBM/pull/284) (test hygiene),
[#285](https://github.com/cxs503/TensorLBM/pull/285) (borrow cache +
distance advisory) and
[#286](https://github.com/cxs503/TensorLBM/pull/286) (the closed-loop
demo) merged 2026-09-08, `main` = `115a33e0`.
