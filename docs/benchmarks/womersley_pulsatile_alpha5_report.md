## 1. Case, geometry and parameters

Harmonic body force `a(t) = a0 cos(omega t)` (i.e. `dp/dx = -rho a`) drives (i) a plane channel of gap `H` between walls at `y = 0.5` and `y = ny - 1.5` and (ii) a circular tube of radius `R` with BFL interpolated bounce-back on the analytic circle. Reference: **Womersley (1955) J. Physiol. 127(3):553-563, doi:10.1113/jphysiol.1955.sp005276** for the tube, **Sexl (1930)** for the parallel-plate form. Womersley number `alpha = R sqrt(omega/nu)` (pipe) / `(H/2) sqrt(omega/nu)` (channel). Lattice units `dx = dt = 1`, `nu = (tau - 1/2)/3`, force scaled so that the analytic centreline peak is `u_peak = 0.03` (`Ma = 0.052`).

## 2. Method

* **channel**: `src/tensorlbm/verification/womersley_pulsatile/channel2d.py`, D2Q9 BGK, pre-streaming half-way bounce-back on the wall rows, periodic in x, second-order Guo (2002) body force (source term transferred from `tensorlbm.ice_hydro.lbm_channel._collide`).
* **pipe**: `.../pipe3d.py`, D3Q19 BGK with Bouzidi-Firdaouss-Lallemand interpolated bounce-back on the true circle (`q<1/2`: `2q f_i(A)+(1-2q) f_i(A-e_i)`; `q>=1/2`: `(1/2q) f_i(A)+(1-1/2q) f_opp(A)`).
* **estimator** (`.../metrics.py`): 64 phase bins over the driving period (whole-period phase averaging; 4 measured periods after `max(5, ceil(6.5 tau_diff/T))` startup periods) plus a least-squares harmonic fit `u = A cos + B sin + C`, `Z = A - iB`. Bin attenuation is sinc(pi/64) = 0.04 % and is negligible against the reported errors.
* **gates** (`.../gates.py`): `TOL_PCT = 5.0` fixed a priori; blocking = centreline amplitude ratio and relative phase lag; informative = full-profile and inner-core phase-averaged L2; separate resolution-refinement check (error must not grow under refinement).
