# 3-D MCMP spurious-current scaling law (`GAP-PP-3-3DMCMP-SPURIOUS-SCALING`)

Campaign home: `benchmarks/mcmp3d_spurious_scaling/`.

| file | role |
|---|---|

## What is measured

A static droplet in a periodic box is the exact null solution of the multiphase
Boltzmann equation (`u ≡ 0`).  Every non-zero velocity the solver leaves behind
is the **spurious (parasitic) current** `u_max`, generated by the discrete
imbalance between the Shan-Chen interaction force and the pressure gradient at a
curved interface (Shan 2006, *Phys. Rev. E* 73, 047701).  `u_max` is the
velocity floor of every 3-D capillary simulation, and the deliverable here is its
dependence on the coexistence density difference `Δρ = ρ_l − ρ_v`:

    u_max = A (Δρ)^p

with the exponent `p` and its OLS uncertainty, over a `(grid × Δρ × forcing)`
matrix.  The forcing axis is the classical velocity shift versus the
Kupershtokh exact-difference method (EDM), so the paper claim can state whether
the scaling is a property of the *model* or of the *forcing*.

## Engine

`tensorlbm.pseudopotential_edm3d` — D3Q19, `float64`, pseudopotential
`ψ = ρ0(1 − e^{−ρ/ρ0})`, BGK `τ = 1`, coexistences from the Maxwell equal-area
construction on the Shan-Chen EOS (`maxwell_coexistence`).  No new solver code
was written for this gap; the deliverable is the measurement protocol, the
statistics, the falsification controls and the artifact chain.

## Acceptance (machine gate, `verdict.json`)

* **C1** `u_max` table over ≥ 3 resolutions × ≥ 3 density ratios;
* **C2** exponent uncertainty `σ_p/|p| < 20 %`;
* **C3** exponent cross-checked between `classic` and `EDM` at ≥ 2 density
  levels (agreement within 2σ);
* **Z/P/M** falsification controls: vanishing intercept at `Δρ → 0`, an `u_max`
  plateau (< 5 % spread over the last three checks), and mass conservation to
  round-off.

`verdict.json` is produced by `fit_report.py`; the pass/fail state of every
criterion in `REPORT.md` is read from it, never typed.

## Reproduce

```bash
python benchmarks/mcmp3d_spurious_scaling/run.py \
    --out artifacts/mcmp3d_spurious_scaling --jobs 30 --threads 2 \
    --steps 5000 --check-every 250 --steady-tol 1e-3
python benchmarks/mcmp3d_spurious_scaling/fit_report.py \
    --results artifacts/mcmp3d_spurious_scaling/results.json
python -m pytest tests/test_mcmp3d_spurious_scaling.py -q
```

The runner prints one line per finished case and writes `results.json` only at
the end (partial campaigns are not silently reported as complete).
